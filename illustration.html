Icons & Illustration Systems

Icons and illustrations for Guiabolso’s app and website: an icon construction guide, and scaling the brand’s illustration system inside the product.

Role

Product Designer & Illustrator

Guiabolso

Tools

50+

Touchpoints: the app, GB Connect (B2B), the website and partner pages

Summary

At Guiabolso I worked as a designer and illustrator before moving into product design. The brand illustrations and their original guide were created by illustrator Lucas Wakamatsu, with our team. My part was making the system work inside the product. I created the icon construction guide for the new design system, replicated illustrations for new product needs, wrote extra usage guides, and built modular character components in Figma.

Later, at Mercado Livre, I took the same approach to write the iconography manual for Pix.

Icon system

For the new design system, I wrote the construction rules for proprietary icons using a simple grid. Within it, I defined the usable area and margins, the basic geometric shapes, and how stroke and corners give the icons their own character. The guide also mapped every old platform icon to its new version, so squads could migrate without guessing.

Illustration system

Building on the original brand guide, I maintained a repository of 50+ illustrations and compositions for product use, replicating the style for new needs, and added usage guides so other teams could apply it consistently across the app, GB Connect (the B2B product), the website and partner landing pages.

Modular components

I turned the characters into modules in Figma, built on shared proportions and basic shapes. Designers could combine bodies, poses and props to compose new illustrations on their own, which kept the style consistent without needing an illustrator.

Applied

The same system across the website marketplace and partner product pages.

Impact

The system gave Guiabolso one recognizable visual language across product and marketing, and let designers on other squads create illustrations within the style. Illustration played a big part in humanizing our flows and, together with the new branding guide, in Guiabolso’s brand awareness.

What I learned

There’s always an exception to the rule, just as people won’t always use the guides (haha).
