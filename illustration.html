Summary

At Guiabolso I worked as a designer and illustrator before moving into product design. The brand illustrations and their original guide were created by illustrator Lucas Wakamatsu, with our team. My part was making the system work inside the product: I created the icon construction guide for the new design system, replicated illustrations for new product needs, wrote extra usage guides, and built modular character components in Figma.

Later, at Mercado Livre, I took the same approach to author the company-wide iconography manual.

Icon system

For the new design system, I wrote the construction rules for proprietary icons: usable area and margins, a base grid, the basic geometric shapes, and how stroke and corners give the icons their own character. The guide also mapped every old platform icon to its new version, so squads could migrate without guessing.

Illustration system

Building on the original brand guide, I maintained a repository of 50+ illustrations and compositions for product use, replicating the style for new needs, and added usage guides so other teams could apply it consistently across the app, GB Connect (the B2B product), the website and partner landing pages.

Modular components

I turned the characters into modules in Figma, built on shared proportions and basic shapes. Designers could combine bodies, poses and props to compose new illustrations on their own, which kept the style consistent without an illustrator in every request.

Applied

The same system across the website marketplace and partner product pages.

Impact

The system gave Guiabolso one recognizable visual language across product and marketing, and let designers on other squads create illustrations within the style.

What I learned

Illustration played a big part in humanizing our flows and in Guiabolso’s brand awareness, but a visual system only scales when other people can use it without you.

Writing the rules down made the process easier between our product team, marketing, and the third-party agencies hired to produce growth content.
